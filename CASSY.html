Cassy is a District 9 local who worked at a Capitol-owned general store run by his family. He was reaped for the 69th Hunger Games as his district's male tribute.


Personality

Before the reaping, Cassy was a sad sack of shit. He found his life mundane and purposeless, taught that what was given to him was all he’ll ever have, even if it sucks cheeks, so he opted to just… lay down and take it. What was the point in doing otherwise? Anyway, Although he was usually pretty friendly and laid-back to others, and sometimes mischevious and sharptongued, but he’s so reserved that he never makes attempts to make friends. He was a bit of a gluttonous mother fucker, prioritizing his comfort and amusement over, yk, being a productive human being. So he can be really lazy some days, or walking laps around town. Really depends what sort of mood he was in. Despite this, he was actually very perceptive, especially when it came to reading people, and was very emotionally intelligent.

After he was reaped, Cassy became much more lively. He was very curious, mischievous, and lowkey hedonistic. He sort of resigned himself to his death, so he wanted to experience/know everything and anything. This meant anything from eating everything that has ever been cooked, to getting laid. He was a bit of an attention whore, and revels in the stardom he found as a tribute. The Capitol thirsts over him, to the point of sexualization sometimes, but Cassy didn't take it seriously. He believed it's all casual fun (uh oh).


Backstory and lore

Cassy was born on May 1st, the year of the 52nd games, to Barbara Wendell and his dad who I don't care enough to name. He was a surprise baby to a teenage couple, and societal expectations make it so they get married and raise the kid to “take responsibility.”

The marriage kinda fell apart immediately due to bitterness on both sides. His Pa became incredibly depressed and tried to escape the district in an effort to run away from it all, but he was caught and hanged. Oh, and he ratted out to the higher ups that Babs’ father had a bastard child and that was so not cool (more on them in Bab’s page). So bye dad, and bye gramps.

From then on, Cass was solely raised by his mother, who began pulling away to focus on her career as a glorified Dollarama manager. She never wanted to be a mother anyways. So, Cass looked at anything to fill the void or something, and gained an interest in Panem as a whole. It was a world unfamiliar to him, and he wanted to see it. To leave his miserable backwater village to shake his routine up a little. The thing is, all his life, he was taught that what he had was all he'd ever get. He’ll die in the same spot he was born in. It was his duty to keep the country from functioning. But Cass found no meaning in his “duty.” It was just sad, plain old monotony. With a shitty boss and shitty customers. He dreamt of breaking away and being his own man… but let's be real broski is NOT getting out. Unless he wants to join the Peacekeeper’s or volunteer for the Hunger Games, but come on be realistic right now.

…UNTILLL he was reaped into the 69th Games!!!!

He was ecstatic. A complete derailing of his boring ass life, carted away to a completely different, luxurious world, surrounded with mostly decent people. Bro, it was everything he ever dreamed of!! You’d think this business would convince him to try winning the Games, but you’d be DEAD WRONG. You underestimated how deep his self-hatred is. Sure he wants a better life, but he believes that his current life is so boring and worthless, some other kid with a real loving family and hopes and dreams deserve to win instead. Plus, aint no fucking way he’d win anyway. Might as well step off the platform and end it before a slower death could get to him.

However, Cassy became tempted to stick around because of the great time he had in the Capitol. Firstly, there was the fame he garnered. The Capitol finds him attractive, and showers him with love at any chance they get. Being someone who never had much positive attention on him, that kind of attention was very flattering. Second, there was his mentor, Aria, and his escort, Dersia. He gets along really well with them, seeing Aria as a fun older sister type and Dersia as a motherly type. If he were to win, they could be his new family. Third, there was the career pack. Seeing his success with the audience and his competence in fighting, the District 1 girl, Silica, invited him in. There, he learned their names. Nik, from D1. Andy and Isiaka, from 2, and Delfie and Altin from 4. He didn’t get along with all of them, but it was enough that he wasn’t on any one of their kill lists.

So, better odds and the guarantee of a good future back at home… Well, considering his life was a total drag up until this moment, Cassy decided “hey, why not give this thing a shot?”
