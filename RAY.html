Ray was the male tribute of District 8 in the 14th Hunger Games. He was close allies with his district mate, Marlowe Vane and lands second place, killed by Donna Shallow.


Personality

Ray is a strong silent type. Stoic, no-nonsense, and serious, and has a very intimidating aura. He's practical to a fault, and can be really bossy, hardly tolerating any tomfoolery, which makes him quite harsh on other people (and himself, of course). He's is incredibly protective and loyal to the people he loves. His family and his gang mean the world to him. Hell, it gets to the point that he defines himself around them. Ray is quite small-minded and selfish. He's kinda like a "burn the whole world for you" kinda dude, which means he's willing to terrorize innocent people (as a part of his gangsta duties) to feed his family. Despite his tough-guy demeanor, Ray is pretty shy. He's fairly confident when he's around his gang, but on his own he's quite awkward, though he tries to hide it by acting nonchalant lol.


Backstory and lore

Ray was born into a poor, somewhat dysfunctional but loving family in District 8 and was the oldest of 4 kids. Things were normal until his mom died when he was 9 and he was forced to take on a parental role to support his overworked dad. He didn’t like it though so he’d slack off a bit. Well until his youngest sibling, Clea, died from an illness while he was out screwing around. It traumatized him into locking in. Shortly after, he joined the city’s local gang for some cash. He ended up working mostly as a debt collector, which molded him into becoming a very tough and intimidating guy.

He ended up working mostly as a debt collector, which molded him into becoming a very tough and intimidating guy.

They beefed nearly the whole training week, but the forces of being in a traumatic situation just kept pulling them together. From tributes that kept trying to bully them, to helping each other learn a few skills they’d need for the arena, and just cause they were both in an unfamiliar situation. Ray was slowly losing his mind from stress, homesickness, and loneliness, so he was craving for companionship. It didn’t truly dawn on him that a companion was right in front of him until the interview tho. So um long story short, Marlowe got into an especially nasty fight after her interview where she got read to absolute filth and burst into tears when Ray was scolding her for it. He was going through his own psychological torment in the interview, being a very camera shy person, and he really needed to feel at home or something. So he kind of impulsively comforted and acted like his usual older bro self as a response to Marlowe’s breakdown, because being someone’s protector was all he knew.

They lowkey never talked about that incident for a while because they were embarrassed, but that night did open their relationship to be a bit more friendly. That probably was a good thing, but it scared the hell out of Ray. He was self-aware enough to know getting attached to Marlowe would make him throw away self-preservation for her, and he couldn’t afford to die cause, yk. Yet as much as he tried to keep his distance, Ray couldn’t stop himself from being distant when his brain was trauma-wired to be emotionally dependent and be someone’s caregiver. At the same time, a part of their strategy in the games was to basically kill everyone on sight. While Ray was not a stranger to violence, he wasn’t used to directing it onto innocent kids. He continued on, deciding it’ll be worth it, but it was eating him alive. That only made him look to Marlowe for comfort, which led to him becoming more attached, which makes him VERY FREAKING MAD AT HIMSELF CAUSE WHAT THE FUCK IS HE DOING. There were times Ray considered just betraying her, even, but he couldn't bring himself to do it.

One day, he suffered a bad concussion while fighting the kids from D10, rendering him totally helpless for a few days. He feared that it would be it for him and fully expected Marlowe to ditch his ass, but she insisted on taking care of him. Ray wasn’t used to be the one who was being taken cared of, which ultimately made him reach to maxx attachment level. And quietly, he decided that he’d do anything to bring Marlowe home. He reasoned that his family had no special use for him that Marlowe couldn’t bring. The most important thing was the prize money, and if Marlowe can bring it home, then that was enough for him. Not like he had a chance at that point with a whole ass brain injury.

But of course absolutely nothing can go right with him when 2 kids they fought earlier came back with a vengeance, wanting to avenge the ally that Ray and Marlowe had murdered. They both managed to win the fight, but not without Ray becoming blinded in one eye and Marlowe was disemboweled (yeowch). She didn’t die immediately though, and she asked him to mercy kill her. As much as he didn’t want to, Ray relented, and he cut her throat.

The next day or two was a miserable blur, but Ray’s resolve to win hardened. It was all up to him now, and he’d stop at nothing. Even if it meant wiping out the entire arena.

On the finale, he ran into 2 tributes: Pavia and Donna, the latter being once allied with the boy who gave him a concussion. They hung out together, but eventually separated on good terms, not wanting to have to kill each other. Ray trailed Pavia, then viciously attacked him. It got Donna’s attention. It was down to both of them now, and they fought like animals. In a cruel, ironic twist, the effects of his concussion began acting up again due to the physical exertion of the fight. He tried to fight it off, but Donna overpowered him and threw him off a cliff.

Ray didn’t die immediately. He laid on the bottom for a few minutes, incapacitated, as he bled out. Looking for any kind of comfort in such a hopeless situation, he hallucinated Marlowe and Livy. They sat with him, uninjured and calm, as his mind slipped away.

Appearance

He's kinda chopped, or at least is considered chopped. Thick bushy eyebrows, an big crooked nose, and a soft jawline. He has curly dark brown hair, dark blue eyes, light olive skin (though he gets pretty tanned in the Games), and a light stubble (not a BEARD as some say...) He is often mistaken for being older than he actually is, around the 18 age range. Though considered average height in his district, he's pretty "short" of around 5'5 (tbf for a guy who was malnourished half his life its not too bad), and has a stocky build.

In terms of clothing, Ray is very plain and pragmatic, dressing up in conventionally masculine ways. His default outfit is a black military shirt as a jacket (GENUINELY stolen), a plain white collared shirt, suspenders, black pants with knee patches, and long worn out boots. He likes to wear a green bandana cowboy-style (idk like, think C!Tommyinnit), which is his marker as a gang member, and is his district token. In his general design, he has bandages over his hands cause his knuckles are usually bruised or bleeding. Take a wild guess why.
